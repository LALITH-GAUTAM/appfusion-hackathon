AppFusion QA fixture 0042
Start with an empty fridge in a fresh QA session.

Scan, review, and confirm
1. Scan grocery-receipt-0042.png. Review shows eggs, spinach, and rice.
2. Change EGGS LRG to Eggs before confirming. If recognition already
   displays Eggs, change the name to Large eggs to exercise the edit.
3. Confirm once, then reopen inventory. Expect exactly three entries,
   one purchased package each of eggs, spinach, and rice. Each entry
   has quantity 1 and a nonempty unit. The saved egg name matches the
   review edit. Prices, totals, and receipt metadata are excluded.
4. Check that the confirm response and GET /api/inventory agree.

Meal acceptance
- Generate a meal only after confirming. POST /api/meals reads the
  saved inventory. The result has a title and actionable steps.
- Spinach and egg fried rice is one acceptable result. The recipe
  should use the confirmed eggs, spinach, and rice and respect their
  amounts. It should say to cook the rice first if starting dry.
- Every ingredient listed as on hand maps to confirmed inventory.
  Anything extra belongs under optional missing ingredients. For
  example, soy sauce may be suggested, and the recipe must work if
  the user skips it.

Failure checks
- Unreadable receipt. Submit a valid image with no readable receipt.
  Expect 422 and guidance to retry. Inventory stays empty.
- Empty fridge. Request a meal before confirmation. Expect 409 and
  guidance to add groceries. The app stays usable.
- Offline. Disconnect with inventory visible, request a meal, then
  reconnect and retry. Expect clear feedback and preserved inventory.
- AI unavailable. While online, induce a meal service error or
  timeout. Expect 503 for a service outage, a loading state that
  ends, preserved inventory, and successful retry after recovery.
- Confirm retry. Let the first confirmation write succeed while
  dropping its response in the QA environment. Retry the same
  receipt and edited items, then reload inventory. Expect exactly
  the same three entries and quantities as after one confirmation.
